Open with the gap in one line: CRC runs 131 public properties, and there’s no single place today that shows them all with what’s at each one. This is a working prototype of that place. It plays on its own, about 20 seconds a stop: the whole city, the red rec centers, a search for “pools”, the Bond Hill Recreation Area’s card, Dunham’s CRC map in place, the fields up close, its printable sheet, the staff record, then back to the city. → (or a clicker) moves ahead, ← goes back, P pauses while you talk, Esc ends. Public and CRC data only · no Google imagery

5

Everything on the map comes from CRC’s records or public data: the property inventory (amenities as of October 2017), CRC’s own amenity maps for the rec centers, City of Cincinnati CAGIS parcels for property lines, and Ohio OSIP aerials, OpenStreetMap and Census TIGER/Line for the base map. No Google imagery. Corrections from CRC staff go straight onto the map and are logged in that site’s staff record. The latest review split Bond Hill into the rec center and the recreation area, updated Mt. Echo’s courts, and put Carll Street on its own land.
